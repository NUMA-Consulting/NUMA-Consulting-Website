Who We Are

A student-powered
marketing consultancy

Founded in 2020, NUMA Consulting (NUMAC) is a student-run marketing consulting agency at Northeastern University dedicated to delivering strategic marketing solutions.


As a subsidiary of the Northeastern University Marketing Association (NUMA), NUMAC specializes in consulting services, partnering with clients to address their marketing challenges and implement impactful strategies — at no cost to you.

"Our consultants bring real-world co-op and internship experience from leading organizations, channeling that expertise into meaningful marketing work for our partners."

Case Studies

Real clients, real results

A closer look at two recent engagements — the kind of hands-on strategy and execution every NUMAC partner can expect.

Matcha Cafe Maiko
Food & Beverage · Winter Revenue Strategy
8 competitors benchmarked 3 seasonal campaigns 580+ IG engagements on one post

A 6-member consulting team helped Matcha Cafe Maiko Boston stabilize winter revenue and deepen engagement with the Northeastern community. The team surveyed Northeastern students and benchmarked 8 competitors on pricing, differentiation, ambiance, and digital presence to uncover growth opportunities.

The team designed and executed 3 seasonal campaigns — a Hoosky collaboration, a Winter Drink Special, and a Finals "Study Break" menu — and built a campus vendor partnership strategy (pricing model, outreach plan, catering framework) to drive bulk orders from student organizations.

"NUMAC felt less like a class project and more like a real consulting team that genuinely cared about helping our business grow." — Henry, Owner of Matcha Cafe Maiko Fenway
R3VIVE Fitness
Health & Wellness · Corporate B2B Growth
5+ competitors analyzed 3 pitch-deck pricing tiers 7 program features recommended

The team benchmarked 5+ corporate wellness providers (including F45, Orange Theory, and Barry's Bootcamp) to help R3VIVE reposition itself as a partner for corporate wellness programs, not just a gym — backed by a full competitive and audience-segmentation report.

The team built a full corporate outreach package — a geo-targeted database of high-fit Boston companies, 4 email templates, and a 3-tier pitch deck ($500–$3,500/month) — plus a 3-month Instagram/LinkedIn content strategy to grow R3VIVE's reach among corporate decision-makers.

"NUMAC brought a level of professionalism, creativity, and strategic thinking that exceeded our expectations and helped elevate our position in the health and wellness space." — Joel, Owner of R3VIVE Fitness
